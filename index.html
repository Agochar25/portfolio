01 · Approach

Where AI meets the person using it

AI only feels useful when the interface makes it legible. I work on both sides: shaping how people interact with a product, and wiring up the models, data and cloud services that make it intelligent.

A

Start from the user's moment

A student checking attendance between classes, or a citizen looking for a scheme they qualify for, needs one clear answer fast. I design the flow around that moment, then decide what the AI should do.

Principle 1 of 3
B

Make AI explain itself

Automated decisions should show their reasoning. Explainable outputs, visible progress and clear states build the trust an AI feature needs before people rely on it.

Principle 2 of 3
C

Ship it for real

A design is finished when it runs on a phone in someone's hand. I build installable apps, real APIs and cloud deployments so ideas can be tested properly.

Principle 3 of 3

Swipe the cards, or tap a principle.
